# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want Quarry UI to be a friendly place for everyone who wants to build, learn or just ask a question, whatever their experience, background, identity or the tools they use. We pledge to keep our spaces welcoming, respectful and free of harassment.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent
- Respect different opinions and different levels of experience
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and say it the way you would want to hear it
- Take feedback gracefully, and say so when you are wrong
- Help newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.

## What we will not accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately derailing a discussion
- Anything else that would be unacceptable in a professional setting

## Where this applies

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, and any place where you represent the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and restrict or ban people who break these rules. We will try to be fair and to explain what happened, but we reserve the right to act without a long debate when someone is being harmful.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, please email **hello@prodhosh.me** with a link or a description of what happened. Reports are handled privately and taken seriously. If the report is about a maintainer, say so, and it will be handled by someone else where possible.
